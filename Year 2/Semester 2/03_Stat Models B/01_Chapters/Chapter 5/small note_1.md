A spurious correlation happens when your model show a 'significant' relationship between variables, but the relationship is not truly meaningful/casual. 



**Eg. 1:** 

Ice-cream sales increase

Drawing incidents increase



**Conc:** Both increase in summer, temperature is the real cause. 



**Eg. 2:** 

Smartphone users increase

Global CO2 increase



**Conc:** Both rise over the years, but they are not directly related. 



**Correlation \\neq causation.**
